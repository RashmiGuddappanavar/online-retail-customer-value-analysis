# Power BI DAX Measure Repository & Specification

## Overview

This repository documents the official DAX measures implemented for the Online Retail II Power BI reporting model. All measures are stored in a dedicated measure table `_Measures` and dynamically calculate metrics from the `Fact_Transactions`, `Dim_Customer`, `Dim_Product`, `Dim_Country`, and `Dim_Date` tables.

---

## 1. Core Financial & Volume Measures

### Measure 1: `Total Revenue`
- **DAX Expression**:
  ```dax
  Total Revenue = 
  CALCULATE(
      SUM(Fact_Transactions[Revenue]),
      Fact_Transactions[IsCancelled] = FALSE(),
      Fact_Transactions[Quantity] > 0,
      Fact_Transactions[UnitPrice] > 0
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Currency `£#,##0.00`
- **Target Value**: **£20,476,034.43**
- **Business Meaning**: Total sales revenue generated from completed transactions (excludes returns, cancellations, and non-positive prices).

---

### Measure 2: `Total Orders`
- **DAX Expression**:
  ```dax
  Total Orders = 
  CALCULATE(
      DISTINCTCOUNT(Fact_Transactions[InvoiceNo]),
      Fact_Transactions[IsCancelled] = FALSE(),
      Fact_Transactions[Quantity] > 0,
      Fact_Transactions[UnitPrice] > 0
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Whole Number `#,##0`
- **Target Value**: **40,067**
- **Business Meaning**: Count of unique completed invoice orders.

---

### Measure 3: `Total Customers`
- **DAX Expression**:
  ```dax
  Total Customers = 
  CALCULATE(
      DISTINCTCOUNT(Fact_Transactions[CustomerID]),
      NOT ISBLANK(Fact_Transactions[CustomerID]),
      Fact_Transactions[IsCancelled] = FALSE()
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Whole Number `#,##0`
- **Target Value**: **5,878**
- **Business Meaning**: Count of unique identified purchasing customers (excludes guest transactions without CustomerID).

---

### Measure 4: `Average Order Value`
- **DAX Expression**:
  ```dax
  Average Order Value = 
  DIVIDE([Total Revenue], [Total Orders], 0)
  ```
- **Format**: Currency `£#,##0.00`
- **Target Value**: **£511.04**
- **Business Meaning**: Mean revenue generated per completed order invoice.

---

## 2. Customer & Retention Metrics

### Measure 5: `Repeat Customers`
- **DAX Expression**:
  ```dax
  Repeat Customers = 
  CALCULATE(
      COUNTROWS(Dim_Customer),
      Dim_Customer[Frequency] > 1
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Whole Number `#,##0`
- **Target Value**: **4,255**
- **Business Meaning**: Count of identified purchasing customers who have placed more than 1 completed order.

---

### Measure 6: `Repeat Customer Rate`
- **DAX Expression**:
  ```dax
  Repeat Customer Rate = 
  DIVIDE([Repeat Customers], [Total Customers], 0)
  ```
- **Format**: Percentage `0.00%`
- **Target Value**: **72.39%**
- **Business Meaning**: Proportion of purchasing customers who are repeat buyers (`4,255 / 5,878`).

---

### Measure 7: `High-Risk Customers`
- **DAX Expression**:
  ```dax
  High-Risk Customers = 
  CALCULATE(
      COUNTROWS(Dim_Customer),
      Dim_Customer[ChurnRiskProxy] = "High Risk (Churn Proxy)"
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Whole Number `#,##0`
- **Target Value**: **1,731**
- **Business Meaning**: Count of customers classified in the High Risk (Churn Proxy) tier (Recency > 90 days AND Frequency >= 2 orders).

---

### Measure 8: `High-Risk Prior Revenue`
- **DAX Expression**:
  ```dax
  High-Risk Prior Revenue = 
  CALCULATE(
      SUM(Dim_Customer[Monetary]),
      Dim_Customer[ChurnRiskProxy] = "High Risk (Churn Proxy)"
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Currency `£#,##0.00`
- **Target Value**: **£2,969,509.67**
- **Business Meaning**: Total historical revenue generated by customers currently classified as high risk for churn.

---

## 3. Data Quality & Cancellation Metrics

### Measure 9: `Cancellation Count`
- **DAX Expression**:
  ```dax
  Cancellation Count = 
  CALCULATE(
      COUNTROWS(Fact_Transactions),
      Fact_Transactions[IsCancelled] = TRUE()
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Whole Number `#,##0`
- **Target Value**: **19,100**
- **Business Meaning**: Count of cancelled transaction line items.

---

### Measure 10: `Cancellation Rate`
- **DAX Expression**:
  ```dax
  Cancellation Rate = 
  DIVIDE([Cancellation Count], COUNTROWS(Fact_Transactions), 0)
  ```
- **Format**: Percentage `0.00%`
- **Target Value**: **1.86%**
- **Business Meaning**: Proportion of total transaction lines that were cancelled (`19,100 / 1,027,071`).

---

## 4. Time Intelligence & Time-Series Measures

### Measure 11: `Previous Month Revenue`
- **DAX Expression**:
  ```dax
  Previous Month Revenue = 
  CALCULATE(
      [Total Revenue],
      DATEADD(Dim_Date[Date], -1, MONTH)
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Currency `£#,##0.00`
- **Business Meaning**: Total sales revenue for the preceding calendar month.

---

### Measure 12: `MoM Revenue Growth %`
- **DAX Expression**:
  ```dax
  MoM Revenue Growth % = 
  VAR CurrentRev = [Total Revenue]
  VAR PriorRev = [Previous Month Revenue]
  RETURN
      IF(
          ISBLANK(PriorRev) || PriorRev = 0,
          BLANK(),
          DIVIDE(CurrentRev - PriorRev, PriorRev, 0)
      )
  ```
- **Format**: Percentage `0.00%`
- **Business Meaning**: Month-over-Month percentage change in completed revenue (returns BLANK for the first month Dec 2009).

---

### Measure 13: `Revenue YTD`
- **DAX Expression**:
  ```dax
  Revenue YTD = 
  TOTALYTD([Total Revenue], Dim_Date[Date])
  ```
- **Format**: Currency `£#,##0.00`
- **Business Meaning**: Cumulative year-to-date completed revenue.

---

## 5. Market Share & Contribution Measures

### Measure 14: `Revenue Contribution %`
- **DAX Expression**:
  ```dax
  Revenue Contribution % = 
  DIVIDE(
      [Total Revenue],
      CALCULATE([Total Revenue], ALLSELECTED(Fact_Transactions)),
      0
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Percentage `0.00%`
- **Business Meaning**: Percentage contribution of current context (e.g. selected country, segment, product) to total portfolio revenue.
